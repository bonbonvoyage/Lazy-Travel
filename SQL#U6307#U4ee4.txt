SQL 加入申請查詢：

SELECT 
    jr.RequestID,
    tg.GroupTitle,
    m.Name AS ApplicantName,
    jr.Message,
    jr.RequestStatus,
    jr.CreatedAt
FROM JoinRequests AS jr
INNER JOIN TravelGroups AS tg
    ON jr.GroupID = tg.GroupID
INNER JOIN Members AS m
    ON jr.MemberID = m.MemberID;




SQL 揪團列表查詢：

SELECT 
    tg.GroupID,
    tg.GroupTitle,
    tg.Description,
    tg.CurrentPeople,
    tg.MaxPeople,
    tg.JoinRule,
    tg.GroupStatus,
    m.Name AS OwnerName
FROM TravelGroups AS tg
INNER JOIN Members AS m
    ON tg.OwnerMemberID = m.MemberID
WHERE tg.IsDelete = 0;
